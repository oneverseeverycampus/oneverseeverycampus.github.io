One Verse. Every Campus.

A simple project sharing Scripture across college campuses.

Each day, one Bible verse is designed to be easily shared and reposted by students and campus ministries across the country.

Our mission is to share daily Scripture through Instagram’s new School Stories feature—campus-wide story feeds where any verified student at a college can see and post content, sort of like a digital bulletin board specific to that campus.

By partnering with students and campus ministries, Bible verses and encouragement are reposted into these shared campus spaces across the nation, with a goal of spreading the Gospel to ALL college students from all walks of life—not just those involved in a church or campus ministry.
