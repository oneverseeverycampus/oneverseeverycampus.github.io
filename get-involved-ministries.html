How to Get Involved: Ministries

As a campus ministry, your involvement looks like:

1. Empowering student leaders.

Encourage students or student leaders to repost verses from the @oneverseeverycampus account to their college’s Instagram School Story.
NOTE: Promotional materials are provided for download below if you would like to spread the word about our mission!

2. Sharing through your ministry account.

Repost verses from the @oneverseeverycampus account on your ministry’s Instagram story whenever it feels appropriate.
