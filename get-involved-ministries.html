How to Get Involved: Ministries

One Verse Every Campus exists through simple, voluntary participation. There is no schedule, quota, or obligation — just a shared desire to make God’s Word visible on college campuses.

As a campus ministry, getting involved is easy and pressure-free.

The most helpful way campus ministries can get involved is by:

1. Empowering student leaders.
Encourage students or student leaders to repost verses from the One Verse Every Campus account to your college’s Instagram School Story.

2. Sharing through your ministry account.
Repost verses from the One Verse Every Campus account on your ministry’s Instagram story whenever it feels appropriate.

In both cases, participation is entirely flexible. Post as often or as rarely as you’d like; the goal is simply to spread the Gospel on campus.
