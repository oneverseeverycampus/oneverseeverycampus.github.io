How to Get Involved

One Verse Every Campus exists through simple, voluntary participation. Whether you’re a student or part of a campus ministry, involvement is easy and pressure-free.

For Campus Ministries

Campus ministries help Scripture reach beyond regular gatherings by reposting verses into campus-wide digital spaces. Participation is flexible and never required.
Ministries can repost verses on their Instagram stories, encourage students to share on their School Stories, or simply engage with the project as it fits their campus context. There is no posting schedule, quota, or obligation.

For Students

Instagram’s School Stories are shared story feeds that anyone at your college can see once they verify their student status. Adding your school allows you to view and post to your campus’s shared story.

Step 1
Open Instagram and go to your profile. Tap the Add school button.
NOTE: If you do not see this option, Instagram likely has your account flagged as a non-student, which has no direct fix. Instagram states: "Teachers, alumni and other adults can't add schools on Instagram."
Step 2
Navigate to Account → School, then enter your official school email address to verify your student status.
Step 3
Once verified, you’ll have access to your campus’s shared School Story. You can repost verses there whenever it feels appropriate by pressing your school's button when posting a story.
